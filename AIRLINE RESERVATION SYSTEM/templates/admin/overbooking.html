Overbooking Policy Formula & Rules

Airlines anticipate statistical passenger no-shows. The system strictly differentiates between Physical Seat Capacity (\( C_{phys} \)) and Maximum Booking Capacity (\( C_{max} \)).

Cmax = ⌊ Cphys × (1 + Overbooking_Percentage / 100) ⌋
